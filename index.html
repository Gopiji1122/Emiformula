A real calculation example

What does a ₹15 lakh loan actually cost?

Here is a simple fixed-rate example using the same kind of calculation our loan tools perform.

Loan amount ₹15,00,000
Annual interest rate 11.5%
Loan tenure 3 years
Estimated monthly EMI ₹49,464

36 monthly payments under a standard reducing-balance calculation.

Loan amount₹15,00,000
Total interest₹2,80,704
Total repayment₹17,80,704
What does this tell us?

You borrow ₹15 lakh, but the scheduled payments add up to about ₹17.81 lakh. The difference is approximately ₹2.81 lakh in interest under these assumptions. That is why we show more than your EMI.

Figures are rounded. This example assumes a fixed 11.5% annual rate, monthly payments and a standard reducing-balance loan. Fees, taxes, insurance and lender-specific charges are not included.

Beyond the EMI

See where your money goes

A monthly payment is only one part of a loan. We show the other numbers that help explain the cost.

Principal

The amount you borrow.

Interest

The borrowing cost calculated over the repayment period.

Total repayment

The combined amount of the scheduled payments.

Outstanding balance

The amount that remains as payments are made.

Payoff time

How long the selected repayment plan takes to clear the balance.

Our approach

We calculate. You decide.

We don't use our calculators to recommend a lender or push you toward a particular financial product. We provide calculations based on the inputs and assumptions shown by each tool.

Actual loan products can differ because of lender policies, fees, taxes, rate changes, payment timing and contractual terms. For important financial decisions, check the actual terms provided by the relevant lender or financial institution.

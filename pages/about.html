About EMIFORMULA

Simple educational tools and explanations for EMI, loans, interest and financial calculations.

About the Creator

My name is Gopal Gorchhiya. I created and developed EMIFORMULA myself. I am a postgraduate in Mathematics, and I use my mathematical knowledge to make financial calculations easier to understand.

Why I Created EMIFORMULA

Before creating EMIFORMULA, I studied financial topics through books, articles, official financial literature, and industry research. I then used my own understanding and mathematical knowledge to organize the information and explain it in my own simple language.

My goal is to help people who may not have much knowledge about the financial sector understand topics such as EMI, loans, interest, repayment, compound interest and investment growth.

Purpose of the Website

EMIFORMULA is created only for educational and informational purposes. The calculators and guides are designed to help users understand mathematical calculations and financial concepts. They are not intended to provide personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Calculators and Accuracy

The calculators use mathematical formulas based on the information entered by the user. Results are estimates. Actual results from a bank, lender, investment provider or other financial institution can be different because their terms, fees, rates, rounding methods, taxes and calculation methods may differ.

I make reasonable efforts to check the formulas, calculations, examples and content. However, minor mathematical, technical, content or human errors may occur.

Financial Information Can Change

Financial rules, interest rates, fees and product conditions can change over time. For important financial decisions, always verify current information with the relevant bank, lender, financial institution or official source.

About EMIFORMULA

Simple tools for everyday financial calculations

EMIFORMULA brings common EMI, loan and financial calculations into one straightforward website.

What EMIFORMULA is

EMIFORMULA is a web-based collection of calculators and educational pages for common EMI, loan and financial calculations. The aim is to make numerical planning easier to understand before a user makes a financial decision.

What you can find here

The site provides interactive tools such as the EMI Calculator, Personal Loan Calculator and Compound Interest Calculator. Guides are designed to explain the numbers in straightforward language.

How to use the information

Calculator results are estimates based on the values entered by the user and the calculation method used by the tool. Actual lender, investment or financial-product terms can differ. Always check the documents and conditions that apply to your specific situation.

Our approach

We aim to keep the interface simple, show the important numbers clearly and explain assumptions where they matter. We also aim to improve the website when calculation, usability or content issues are identified.

A practical limitation

EMIFORMULA is an informational website. It is not a bank, lender, investment adviser, tax adviser or financial institution, and the website does not arrange or approve financial products.

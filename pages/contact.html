Contact

For website questions, feedback, correction requests or reports of a broken page, use the contact method published by the site owner for this website.

If a dedicated email address is added later, this page can be updated without changing the calculator systems.

What to include in a useful message

  • The page or calculator you were using.
  • A short description of the problem or question.
  • The device or browser involved, when reporting a technical issue.
  • Relevant screenshots or error text when available.

Financial questions

EMIFORMULA can explain how a calculator works, but it cannot review your personal finances or tell you which financial product you should choose. For personal financial advice, contact an appropriately qualified professional.

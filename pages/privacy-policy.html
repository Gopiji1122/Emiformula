Privacy Policy

This page explains the general privacy approach for EMIFORMULA and the information users should avoid entering into calculator fields.

Information you provide

EMIFORMULA is designed to perform calculations in the browser. Do not enter passwords, banking credentials, card numbers, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information into calculator fields.

Calculator inputs

Values entered into a calculator are used to produce the requested calculation in the page. The exact handling of technical data can also depend on the hosting platform, browser, analytics, security and other services used by the website.

Cookies and third-party services

Depending on how the website is configured, hosting, analytics, advertising or other third-party services may use technical information or cookies according to their own policies. This page should be reviewed and updated if such services are added or changed.

External links

EMIFORMULA may link to external websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices and terms. Review the relevant third-party policy before submitting personal information.

Updates

This privacy page may be revised when the website, its features or its supporting services change. The current version published on this page is the version that applies to the website at that time.

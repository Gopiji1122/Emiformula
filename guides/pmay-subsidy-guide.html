What is the PMAY-Urban 2.0 Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)?

Under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0, the central government supports urban families through four verticals: Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC), Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP), Affordable Rental Housing (ARH), and the Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS).

If you are taking a home loan from a bank or housing finance company (HFC) to purchase or construct your first pucca house in an urban area, the ISS vertical provides a 4.0% per annum interest subsidy on the first ₹8,00,000 of your loan for a tenure up to 12 years.

Official MoHUA Baseline (Effective 01 September 2024)

• Maximum Gross Subsidy Released: ₹1,80,000 (paid in 5 equal annual installments of ₹36,000 each).

• Net Present Value (NPV) Cap: ₹1,50,000 calculated at an official 8.5% discount rate.

• Loan Sanction Cutoff: Only home loans sanctioned and disbursed on or after 1 September 2024 qualify.

7 Mandatory Eligibility Conditions for PMAY-U 2.0

Your family and property must satisfy every condition below at the time of loan sanction:

  • Household Income Cap (₹9 Lakh): Total annual gross income of husband, wife, and unmarried children must be within ₹9,00,000. EWS (up to ₹3L), LIG (₹3L–₹6L), and MIG (₹6L–₹9L) all receive the same subsidy rate.
  • No Pucca House in India: Neither the applicant nor any family member may own an all-weather pucca house anywhere in India. (An adult earning son or daughter is treated as an independent household).
  • Property Value Ceiling (₹35 Lakh): The total cost of the house or flat cannot exceed ₹35,00,000. Even ₹36 lakh disqualifies the application completely.
  • Maximum Home Loan (₹25 Lakh): Sanctioned home loan must not exceed ₹25,00,000. While subsidy is computed on the first ₹8 lakh, the remaining loan up to ₹25 lakh runs at normal bank interest.
  • Carpet Area Limit (120 sq. m.): Maximum carpet area of the dwelling unit is capped at 120 square metres (~1,291 square feet).
  • Minimum Loan Tenure (> 5 Years): Your home loan tenure must be more than 5 years so all 5 annual installments can be disbursed.
  • Mandatory Female Ownership: The property deed must have the female head of household as sole owner or co-owner with her husband (exempted only if there is no adult female in the family).

How the ₹1.80 Lakh Subsidy & Savings Are Calculated

The government calculates interest savings at 4% p.a. on up to ₹8,00,000 over 12 years and discounts those monthly cash flows at 8.5% p.a. to arrive at an NPV of ₹1,50,000. Because the payout is spread across 5 annual installments rather than day one, the total gross amount released is ₹1,80,000 (₹36,000 × 5).

Annual Credit = ₹36,000 × 5 Years = ₹1,80,000 Total Principal Reduction
Worked Example: ₹20 Lakh Loan at 8.50% for 20 Years

• Regular Monthly EMI: ₹17,356 | Regular Total Interest: ₹21,65,552

• With 5 Yearly Credits of ₹36,000: Each ₹36,000 directly knocks down your remaining principal at Months 12, 24, 36, 48, and 60.

• Result (Tenure Reduction Mode): Loan closes in 202 months instead of 240 months (38 months earlier), saving ₹4,88,200 in bank interest on top of the ₹1,80,000 subsidy—a combined financial benefit of ₹6,68,200.

The 5-Installment Rule & 50% Principal Condition

Under PMAY-Urban 2.0 guidelines (para 5.4.10), each of the 5 annual ₹36,000 installments is released by the Central Nodal Agency (National Housing Bank or HUDCO) to your lender only if two conditions hold on the release date:

  1. Active Loan Account: Your home loan must not be closed or classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA).
  2. More Than 50% Principal Outstanding: At the time each installment is released, more than 50% of your original sanctioned loan principal must still be outstanding. Avoid making large part-prepayments that push your balance below 50% during the first 5 years.

Step-by-Step: How to Apply Online on the Unified Portal

You do not need to pay any middleman or agent. Follow the official 5-step process:

  1. Visit Official Portal: Open pmay-urban.gov.in and click on "Apply for PMAY-U 2.0".
  2. Aadhaar e-KYC: Choose the Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) vertical and verify your Aadhaar number via OTP.
  3. Fill Household & Income Details: Enter family member Aadhaar details, annual household income, and select your lending bank (Primary Lending Institution - PLI).
  4. Submit & Save Application ID: Submit the online form and download your unique PMAY-U 2.0 Application ID.
  5. Hand Over ID to Your Bank: Give this Application ID to your home loan officer. The bank verifies your property documents, uploads the claim to NHB/HUDCO, and credits each ₹36,000 installment to your loan account.

Documents Required for PMAY-Urban 2.0 ISS

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card (mandatory for applicant and all family members) and PAN Card.
  • Income Proof: Self-declaration / Income Affidavit for EWS/LIG/MIG, plus salary slips, Form-16, or ITR as required by your bank.
  • Property Documents: Registered Agreement to Sale / Sale Deed showing property cost ≤ ₹35 lakh, carpet area ≤ 120 sq. m., and female ownership.
  • No-Pucca-House Undertaking: Signed affidavit confirming no family member owns a pucca house in India.
  • Loan Sanction & Bank Details: Home loan sanction letter dated on or after 01-09-2024.

FAQ

Is the ₹1.80 lakh subsidy transferred to my savings account?

No. The subsidy is never paid in cash to a savings account. It is credited in 5 yearly installments directly to your home loan account to reduce your principal balance.

What if my house price is ₹36 lakh or loan is ₹26 lakh?

You will not qualify. Both the ₹35 lakh property value cap and ₹25 lakh loan cap are hard eligibility ceilings under MoHUA PMAY-U 2.0 guidelines.

Can I sell my house after receiving the PMAY 2.0 subsidy?

There is a mandatory 5-year lock-in period from the date of first loan disbursement during which the subsidized house cannot be sold or transferred.

Does PMAY-U 2.0 cover loans taken only to buy a residential plot?

No. Loans taken solely to purchase land or an empty plot do not qualify. The loan must be for purchasing a ready/under-construction flat or constructing a house on owned land.

Which nodal agencies track PMAY-U 2.0 subsidy status?

National Housing Bank (NHB), Housing and Urban Development Corporation (HUDCO), and State Bank of India act as Central Nodal Agencies (CNAs) for disbursing ISS subsidies.

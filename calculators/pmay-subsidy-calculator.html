Key Rules of PMAY-Urban 2.0 (2024–2029)

How is PMAY-U 2.0 different from the old CLSS scheme?

The old Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) expired in March 2022 and offered up to ₹2.67 lakh upfront. PMAY-Urban 2.0 (effective 1 September 2024) offers a unified 4% interest subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh of a loan across EWS, LIG, and MIG (income up to ₹9 lakh), capped at ₹1.80 lakh released over 5 yearly installments.

Why is the subsidy ₹1.80 Lakh when the NPV is ₹1.50 Lakh?

A 4% interest subsidy on ₹8 lakh over 12 years discounted at 8.5% gives a Net Present Value (NPV) of ₹1.50 lakh. Because the government releases the money across 5 yearly installments instead of day one, the actual cash released to your loan account is ₹1.80 lakh (₹36,000 × 5).

What is the 50% principal outstanding rule?

Before releasing each yearly ₹36,000 installment through NHB or HUDCO, the system checks that your home loan account is active and at least 50% of the original sanctioned principal is still outstanding. Avoid aggressive prepayments below 50% until Year 5 finishes.

How do I apply for the subsidy?

Register your demand on the official Unified Web Portal (pmay-urban.gov.in) using Aadhaar authentication under the Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) vertical, then share your generated Application ID with your lending bank or HFC.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do I get ₹1.80 lakh cash in my savings bank account?

No. The government never transfers cash into your savings account. Each ₹36,000 installment is credited directly into your home loan account to reduce your outstanding principal balance.

If my flat costs ₹38 lakh, can I still get subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh?

No. The ₹35 lakh property value and ₹25 lakh loan amount are strict eligibility cutoffs. If your property value exceeds ₹35 lakh or your loan exceeds ₹25 lakh, the loan is disqualified from PMAY-U 2.0.

Are EWS, LIG, and MIG given different subsidy amounts in PMAY 2.0?

No. Unlike phase 1, PMAY-Urban 2.0 applies the exact same benefit formula to all three groups: EWS (up to ₹3L), LIG (₹3L–₹6L), and MIG (₹6L–₹9L) all receive a 4% subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh (up to ₹1.80 lakh).

Can an unmarried earning son or daughter apply separately?

Yes. According to MoHUA guidelines, an adult earning member (irrespective of marital status) is treated as a separate household, provided they do not own a pucca house in their name anywhere in India.

Is female ownership compulsory for PMAY-Urban 2.0?

Yes. The house purchased or constructed must be in the name of the female head of the household or jointly with her husband. Male-only ownership is permitted only when there is no adult female member in the family.

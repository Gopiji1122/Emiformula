How to use the Balance Transfer Break-Even Calculator

Start with the outstanding balance and remaining tenure shown by your current lender. Then enter the new lender's rate and proposed tenure. Finally, add the actual switching charges shown in your lender documents.

Why the break-even month matters

A lower rate does not automatically mean a cheaper transfer. Processing charges, applicable taxes and prepayment costs can delay or eliminate the benefit. The calculator therefore compares the two repayment paths month by month.

Regulatory note

RBI rules on pre-payment charges depend on the loan's rate type, borrower/purpose, lender and applicable directions. The calculator does not treat every floating-rate loan as automatically fee-free. Verify the charge in your lender's current statement, Key Facts Statement or sanction documents.

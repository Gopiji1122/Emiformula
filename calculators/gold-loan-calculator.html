Gold Loan Guide

How a gold loan is calculated

A lender generally assesses the eligible gold weight and purity, applies its valuation price, and then applies the applicable loan-to-value limit to estimate the maximum amount that can be sanctioned. Jewellery stones and other non-gold components are not treated as gold value.

Gold weight, purity and jewellery valuation

Gross jewellery weight is not always the same as eligible gold weight. Stones, gems, fittings and other non-gold materials can reduce the net gold content considered for valuation. The lender's assaying and valuation process determines the final eligible amount.

Gold loan LTV explained

For applicable consumption loans under the RBI's 2025 harmonised framework, maximum LTV ceilings are 85% for loans up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% for loans above ₹2.5 lakh and up to ₹5 lakh, and 75% for loans above ₹5 lakh. These are regulatory ceilings, not a promise that a lender will sanction the maximum amount.

Gold price used for valuation

This calculator asks you to enter the current gold price per gram. The lender may use its prescribed valuation method and price benchmark, so its final valuation can differ from the rate you enter here.

What happens at the lender?

The lender may verify KYC and other eligibility requirements, assay the pledged jewellery, record gross and net weight and purity, value the eligible gold, determine the applicable LTV, document the pledge and then disburse the loan under the agreed product terms.

Repayment, charges and default

Gold loans can have different repayment structures, including EMI or other lender-specific arrangements. Processing, valuation, documentation, interest and other charges can vary. If the borrower does not repay according to the agreement, the lender may take recovery or auction action under applicable rules and the loan agreement.

Calculator limitations

This calculator is an estimate only. It does not verify your gold, determine actual purity, know a lender's internal valuation, guarantee approval, calculate every lender-specific fee or model every repayment product. The lender's final valuation, sanction letter and agreement control.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much gold loan can I get for my gold?

The calculator estimates the maximum loan amount from your entered gold weight, purity, current gold price and the applicable LTV assumption. The lender makes the final valuation and sanction decision.

Should I enter jewellery weight with stones?

For a more accurate estimate, enter gold weight without stones, diamonds or other non-gold materials. If you only know the total jewellery weight, the result is only an estimate because the lender may determine a lower eligible net gold weight.

Which gold price should I enter?

Enter the current gold price per gram that you want to use for the estimate. The lender may use its prescribed valuation method and price, which can differ from the rate you enter.

Can I enter gold weight in tola?

Yes. Select tola as the weight unit. The calculator converts to grams using 1 tola ≈ 11.6638 grams.

Is the calculated loan amount guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate, not a loan offer. Final approval depends on the lender’s valuation, LTV, product rules, eligibility checks and agreement terms.

How much gold do I need for a specific loan amount?

Choose “I need a loan”, enter the desired loan amount, current gold price and purity. The calculator estimates the gold weight required under the applicable LTV assumption.

PM Surya Ghar Mathematical Formula, CFA Subsidy Slabs & 7% Loan Amortization Explained

Under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) operational guidelines for PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (verified as of November 11, 2026), residential households installing grid-connected rooftop solar plants through the National Portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) receive Central Financial Assistance (CFA) calculated according to a two-tier piecewise mathematical formula:

1. Official MNRE Central Subsidy (CFA) Piecewise Formula

For residential rooftop solar systems of capacity C (in kW):

  • For 0 < C ≤ 2 kW: Subsidy = C × ₹30,000 (e.g., ₹30,000 for 1 kW; ₹60,000 for 2 kW).
  • For 2 kW < C ≤ 3 kW: Subsidy = ₹60,000 + (C − 2) × ₹18,000 (reaching ₹78,000 at 3 kW).
  • For C > 3 kW (e.g., 4 kW to 10 kW): Subsidy = ₹78,000 (Maximum Statutory Cap).

This direct benefit transfer (DBT) is credited directly to the consumer's bank account within 30 days of net-meter commissioning by the state DISCOM.

2. Concessional 7.0% Solar Bank Loan & Reducing-Balance EMI Formula

For systems up to 3 kW (loan requirement up to ₹2,00,000), public sector banks offer collateral-free financing at 0.50% above the RBI Repo Rate (currently ~7.00% p.a.) with a 10% borrower margin contribution on net cost after subsidy. The monthly installment is computed via the standard banking reducing-balance formula:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n − 1], where P = Total Benchmark Cost − MNRE Subsidy − Borrower Down Payment, r = Annual Rate / 1200, and n = Loan Tenure in Months.

3. Worked Example: 3 kW Residential Rooftop Solar System (2026 Benchmark)

At an indicative MNRE benchmark cost of ₹1,45,000 for a 3 kW plant: subtracting the ₹78,000 central subsidy leaves a net cost of ₹67,000. With a 10% borrower margin (₹6,700), the financed bank loan is ₹60,300. At 7.0% p.a. over 5 years (60 months), your monthly EMI is ₹1,194, while a 3 kW plant generates ~360 units/month—saving ₹2,700/month (at ₹7.50/unit) and creating a positive net monthly cash flow of +₹1,506 from Month 1.

Frequently Asked Questions (PM Surya Ghar 2026)

How is the ₹78,000 central subsidy credited?

The central government subsidy is transferred directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into your linked bank account within 30 days after DISCOM inspection and net meter installation.

What are the interest rate and terms for PM Surya Ghar bank loans?

Leading public sector banks (SBI, Canara Bank, PNB, Bank of Baroda) offer collateral-free rooftop solar loans up to ₹2 lakh at a concessional repo-linked rate of 7.00% p.a. with tenures up to 10 years (120 months).

Can I install a 5 kW or 10 kW system?

Yes. However, the central residential subsidy is capped at ₹78,000 (which covers the first 3 kW). For capacities above 3 kW, you pay the benchmark commercial rate (~₹45,000 per additional kW) without extra central subsidy.

How much roof area is required for 1 kW solar?

A standard 1 kW rooftop solar system using high-efficiency Mono PERC or TopCon panels requires approximately 100 sq. ft. (about 9.3 sq. metres) of shadow-free roof area.

What happens to extra units generated on sunny days?

Through Net Metering, surplus electricity generated during daytime is exported to the power grid. Your DISCOM credits these units against your nighttime or monsoon electricity consumption.

PPF calculator guide

Use this estimate to plan, not as an official account statement.

How to use this calculator

Choose New account or Existing account. Enter the planned deposit and select Monthly or Yearly. Choose a rate and total account term. For an existing account, enter the current balance and completed financial years. Review the yearly table before using the estimate for a decision.

How the estimate is worked out

The calculator applies the rate you enter month by month. Deposits marked on or before the 5th are included before that month’s estimated interest; deposits after the 5th are added after it. Interest is added to the projected balance at the end of each financial year. Actual account interest depends on the official rules and real transaction dates.

Why the 5th matters

For PPF, the monthly interest calculation uses the lowest balance between the close of the 5th day and the end of that month. A deposit made after the 5th may not earn interest for that month. The difference shown is an estimate based on your chosen deposit pattern.

How maturity works

The initial 15-year period is counted from the end of the financial year in which the account was opened, not simply 15 years from the opening day. Confirm your exact maturity date with your bank or post office.

Already have a PPF account?

Enter the current balance and the number of completed financial years. The calculator projects forward from the balance you provide. It does not know your old deposits, withdrawals or past interest, so it cannot rebuild the exact past account history.

Can the interest rate change?

Yes. The government may revise PPF rates. This estimate applies the rate currently entered to the future projection and does not predict future changes. For a more realistic long-term plan, compare more than one rate.

After 15 years

PPF extension choices have conditions. Continuing without deposits and extending with deposits are different options, and withdrawal rules may differ. Check the current official scheme rules before making an extension decision.

FAQ

Does this show the exact amount I will receive?

No. It is an estimate based on the inputs and assumed rate. Actual interest depends on official rates, account transactions and scheme rules.

Why do I need to enter my current balance?

For an existing account, the current balance already includes past deposits and interest. The calculator uses it as the starting point for future estimates.

Why can depositing by the 5th make a difference?

PPF monthly interest uses the lowest balance between the close of the 5th and month-end. A later deposit may miss interest for that month.

Can I deposit more than ₹1.5 lakh per year?

The usual annual deposit limit is ₹1.5 lakh. This calculator caps the estimate at that limit; check current official rules for your account.

Why are 20, 25, 30 and 35 years available?

They represent the initial 15-year term plus five-year extension blocks. Actual extension eligibility and choices depend on scheme rules.

Can I use this to calculate past interest exactly?

No. A current balance alone does not include the full history of deposits and withdrawals needed to reproduce exact past interest.

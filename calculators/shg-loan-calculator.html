FAQ

Is this an official SHG loan eligibility checker?

No. CCL and corpus results are indicative planning estimates only. A bank and the relevant SHG federation or mission determine eligibility and sanction under current rules.

What does ₹1 per hundred per month mean?

It means 1% interest per month, equivalent to a nominal 12% per year before considering how the balance reduces.

Why does interest change each month?

Under reducing-balance methods, interest is calculated on the unpaid principal at the start of each month.

Does the gross interest spread equal group profit?

No. It excludes fees, timing differences, defaults, collection delays, operating expenses and any differences in actual bank drawdown or repayment.

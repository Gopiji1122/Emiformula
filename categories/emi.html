Use these calculators to understand regular loan payments and see how changing the payment pattern can affect interest, repayment time and outstanding balance.

Choose the EMI calculation that matches your question

An EMI is the scheduled payment used to repay a loan through interest and principal over time. A standard EMI calculation is useful when the payment stays broadly unchanged. If your payment is expected to change, the Step-Up / Step-Down calculator lets you model that change instead of treating the loan as a fixed-payment plan.

Which EMI calculator should you use?

Use the standard calculator when you want a straightforward estimate from three core loan inputs. Use the Step-Up / Step-Down calculator when your payment may increase or decrease during the loan. For either approach, the result depends on the interest rate, repayment period and timing of each payment change.

These tools are calculation aids rather than lender quotations. Actual loan terms can differ because lenders may use different interest conventions, fees, rounding rules or repayment schedules.

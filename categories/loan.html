What to compare before changing a loan

Look beyond the new EMI. A lower monthly payment can result from a longer tenure, while fees and prepayment costs can reduce or eliminate the benefit of a lower rate. Compare the remaining interest on the current loan with the interest and one-time costs of the proposed loan.

The calculator results are estimates based on the inputs supplied. Lender-specific charges, tax treatment, rounding and contractual terms should be checked against the actual offer documents.
